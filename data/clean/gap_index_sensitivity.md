## How to read this

- **Overlap** counts how many of the baseline top-10 districts stay in the top 10 when the method changes.
- **Spearman ρ** compares the full ranking of all districts (1 = identical order).
- Removing a component tests whether one data source drives the result; ±50% weights test the arbitrary equal-weight choice.
- Risk, poverty and the distance-problem indicator are province values (DHS), so districts in the same province share them; travel time, facilities and population vary by district.
