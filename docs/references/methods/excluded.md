# Not built (by design)

| Excluded | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cancer risk prediction** (lung, breast, liver, colorectal…) | No risk tool has been validated for Cambodia. A low score could falsely reassure someone who needs screening or care. Cervical cancer is covered only as a *screening reminder* from the WHO guideline, not as a risk score. |
| **Individual infectious-disease prediction** (other than the TB symptom screen) | No validated individual-level tools that fit a self-check. TB is the exception because the symptom screen is an official WHO / Cambodian programme practice that leads to a *free test*. |
| **Anything needing genetic data, imaging or data users don't have** | Users can't provide it reliably, and results would be guesses. |
| **AI-based risk calculation of any kind** | Rule 1: every score is a published tool implemented as a pure function. AI only explains a finished result. |

Candidates researched in Phase 2 and recommended **for later** (not excluded forever) are listed in `PHASE2_SUMMARY.md`: SCORED (CKD questionnaire), STOP-Bang and OSTA.
